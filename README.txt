Classic 95 Retro Cursor — portfolio update

This package is based on your MOST RECENT retro-portfolio-motion-v2 version, which included your latest text edits.

WHAT CHANGED:
- Classic white Windows-style pointing hand now replaces the normal pointer inside the site on desktop.
- Removed teal companion cursor and its JavaScript tracking.
- Kept click sparkles, staggered chips, project hover effects, typewriter name, scroll reveals, project popups.
- No changes to your content, social links, or CV paths.

INSTALL:
1. Copy index.html, styles.css, and script.js over the existing files in your portfolio.
2. Copy assets/cursors/finger-classic.png to your existing portfolio's assets/cursors folder.
3. KEEP your current assets/icons, image, and real PDF files; they're intentionally not replaced.
4. Refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac).

The cursor icon is 56x68 pixels. Its hotspot is defined at 24 4 near the raised fingertip.
The CSS applies it to desktop/fine-pointer devices. Touch devices use their normal behavior.
If your browser does not support the custom cursor image, a pointer fallback is used.

Tip: To remove the hand from non-clickable areas later, narrow the CSS selector
at the bottom of styles.css to links/buttons and other interactive elements.
